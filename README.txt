IMPORTANT ABOUT MUSIC AUTOPLAY
Browsers generally block audible autoplay until a visitor interacts with the page. The opening cover's Open Invitation tap is used as that interaction and the Spotify embed is loaded with autoplay requested. Spotify/browser/device settings may still require the visitor to tap Play in the embedded player. A forced, guaranteed autoplay cannot be promised.

PUBLISH WITH A CUSTOM DOMAIN
1. Extract the ZIP archive.
2. Upload the extracted folder to a static host such as Netlify.
3. Register/connect your custom domain in the hosting dashboard.
4. In index.html, replace YOUR-DOMAIN-HERE in the og:image and twitter:image metadata with your actual domain (for example, https://example.com). Social apps use these tags to show the link preview image.
5. Follow the DNS instructions from your domain registrar/host and wait for HTTPS to become active.
6. Share the live HTTPS URL. Use the WhatsApp link-preview debugger/cache refresh if WhatsApp or another app continues showing an old preview.
7. Scan and test the QR code after publishing; it must open your live domain.
